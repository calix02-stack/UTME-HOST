How to play

  • Play a card that matches the top card's shape or number.
  • Can't play? Tap the Market to draw one card. Your turn then ends.
  • 1 Hold on: play again. 8 Suspension: play again.
  • 2 Pick two. 5 Pick three. If defend is on, you can answer with the same number to pass it back.
  • 14 General market: the other player draws 1 and you play again.
  • 20 Whot plays on anything and lets you choose the shape.
  • First to finish all cards wins.
