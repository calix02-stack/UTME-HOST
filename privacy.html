Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction: This Privacy Policy explains how MyUTME ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and website (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect: we collect your username, email address, and exam performance data (scores, answers submitted, and weak subject areas) when you create an account and use the app. We may also collect basic technical data such as device type and app usage statistics to help us maintain and improve the Service.

Analytics: we may use standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) to understand how the app is used, in order to improve performance and features. These tools may collect anonymized usage data such as screens viewed and session length.

Payments: card and payment details are handled entirely by Paystack, a licensed third-party payment processor. We never see or store your card number - we only receive a payment reference and status.

How we use your data: to create and run your account, track your exam progress, display you on leaderboards (using your username only), send notifications about your activation status, and improve the Service through aggregated, anonymized insights.

Sharing: we do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We only share data with service providers who help us run the Service, currently limited to Paystack (payment processing), Supabase (database hosting), and analytics providers as described above. These providers are only permitted to use your data to provide services to us.

Data retention: we retain your account and performance data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records by law (e.g. payment records).

Account deletion: you can request full deletion of your account and associated data at any time from your Profile page, or by contacting us via the Support Chat in the app or on WhatsApp. We will process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's privacy: MyUTME is an educational exam-preparation app intended for students preparing for UTME, which may include users under 18. We do not knowingly collect more personal information than necessary from any user, and we encourage parents/guardians to supervise use of the app by younger students. We do not knowingly permit account creation by children under 13 without appropriate parental involvement. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data inappropriately, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data security: we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights: depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can exercise these rights at any time from your Profile page, or by contacting support.

Changes to this policy: we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact: for privacy questions or data requests, use the Support Chat in the app or reach us on WhatsApp.
